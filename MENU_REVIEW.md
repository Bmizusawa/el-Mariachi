# Menu Review — El Mariachi (Prattville, AL)

All menu items were transcribed from the eight photographed pages of the
spiral-bound printed menu (`image0.jpeg` – `image7.jpeg`). Printed menu
is the source of truth. The saved Google Search page contains a DoorDash
menu snapshot used only as a secondary cross-reference.

## Verbatim notices confirmed from the printed menu

- Cover (image0): "Extras and/or substitutions are subject to an extra
  charge. $0.25 will be added to each item on to-go orders."
- Cover (image0): "All card payments have a 3.5% fee added."
- Combinations page (image4): "First order of chips & salsa is free with
  purchase of a lunch or dinner entrée. Extra chips: $1.00 each.
  Extra salsa: $0.75 each"
- Health advisory (image2/image4): "Consuming raw or undercooked meat,
  poultry, seafood, shell-fish or eggs may increase your risk of food
  borne illness, especially if you have certain medical conditions."
- Drinks page (image6): "All drinks must be paid once ordered, no
  exchanges or refunds" · "All drinks contain 1.25% alcohol Unless on
  special request" (odd wording preserved verbatim — likely means a
  1.25 oz pour; owner confirmation recommended) · "Must be 21 or older
  to order a drink"
- Lunch Menu (image7): "Monday through Friday · 11am to 2pm"
- Kids Meals (image7): "Drink included $7.75. For children 12 and under.
  Add $2.00 for adult portion." ($7.75 base / $9.75 adult portion)
